Alerts & e-mail notifications

Banks score their live transactions with the consortium model and report only aggregate counts and patterns here. Case details stay inside each bank, which respects the AML tipping-off rule and GDPR minimisation. Serious events also trigger an e-mail: quarantine, an exfiltration attempt, a forged update, a ledger integrity failure, or a bank reporting 3 or more high-risk transactions in a round.
