Audit ledger

An append-only hash chain. Each record stores the previous record's hash and is signed by the operator key, and each round is sealed with a Merkle root. The ledger holds metadata only: no transactions, labels or model weights.

Press “Verify chain” to recompute every hash and signature.

Integrity check

Recomputes all hashes, links and Ed25519 signatures and stops at the first bad record.
