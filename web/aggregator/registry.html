Participant registry & access control

A bank joins with a request signed by its own Ed25519 key. Only the operator can approve or revoke a bank, and every decision is written to the audit ledger. The ConsortiumRegistry smart contract is the source of truth: the aggregator accepts an update only if the contract says the bank and its key are authorised, and it anchors every round's Merkle root on-chain.

Access rules enforced by the aggregator

  1. A registration must be signed by the key it declares, which proves the bank owns that key.
  2. A bank ID can never be re-registered with a different key, which blocks impersonation.
  3. Pending, revoked or quarantined banks receive no model and their updates are refused.
  4. Every accepted update must carry a valid signature from the registered key.
  5. Two anomalous rounds, any exfiltration attempt or a forged update lead to automatic quarantine.
  6. Only the operator can reinstate a bank, and that decision is logged.
  7. The regulator account can revoke or quarantine a bank directly on the contract, but cannot approve one.
