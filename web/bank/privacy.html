Privacy budget

With differential privacy on, every gradient step is clipped and noised (DP-SGD). The Rényi-DP accountant converts the noise level, sampling rate and number of steps into ε. A smaller ε means stronger privacy. The consortium sets a cap, and this node stops training once its next round would exceed the cap.

What ε means here

ε bounds how much the shared model can reveal about any single transaction of this bank. With ε = 3 and δ = 10⁻⁵, adding or removing one transaction changes the probability of any training outcome by a factor of at most e³ ≈ 20, except with a probability of 10⁻⁵.
