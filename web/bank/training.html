Local training

Each round, this node receives the global model, checks its hash, evaluates it on local test data, and trains locally with FedProx and optional DP-SGD. It then signs the new weights with its Ed25519 key and sends them. Only the weights, the sample count and the privacy budget spent are transmitted.
